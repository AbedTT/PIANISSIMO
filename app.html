Quick start

  1. Pick a length: 1…7 (whole … 64th), . for dotted
  2. Type notes A–G; 0 for a rest
  3. ↑↓ move the note, = - sharp / flat
  4. Shift+letter builds chords
  5. I to click notes in with the mouse
  6. Shift+arrows select; Ctrl+C/V copy & paste
  7. Shift+3 triplets, S slur, [ ] repeats
  8. Ctrl+L lyrics, Ctrl+K chord symbols
  9. V switches to a second voice on the same staff
  10. Change instruments under Sounds
  11. Alt+A plays the chords as a band; L loops
  12. Space to listen
