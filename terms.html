Terms of use

The short version: your music is yours. Be respectful with what you share.

Your content

You keep all rights to the music you write. By making a score unlisted or public you allow others to view and play it through Pianissimo, to copy it into their own editor, and to remix it: make their own version, which may be shared with credit to your original. If you do not want that, keep the score private.

What you may share and write

  • Share and scan only music you wrote, music in the public domain, or music you have permission to use.
  • Do not share content or write comments that are unlawful, hateful, harassing or that infringe someone else's rights.
  • Anyone can report a comment or a shared score. Reported content may be hidden automatically and reviewed by the site's admins, who may remove comments or make scores private.

Classes

Teachers are responsible for the classes they create and the assignments they set. Students' work in a class can be opened, commented on and edited by the teacher.

The service

Pianissimo is provided as is, without warranties. Keep your own backups of important scores: use Download, MusicXML export, or Download my data. You can delete your account at any time from the account menu.
