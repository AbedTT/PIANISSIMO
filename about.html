Making sheet music should feel as easy as writing a text message.

Why Pianissimo

Most notation software makes you hunt through palettes and menus. Pianissimo is built around the keyboard: every action is one short command, so writing a melody feels like typing a sentence, and you hear it as you go.

It is for students writing their first exercises, teachers preparing worksheets, songwriters sketching lead sheets, and choirs printing parts.

How it works

  • Everything runs in your browser and there is nothing to install. Once the editor is open you can keep writing without a connection; instrument sounds download the first time you use them.
  • Scores use an open format and export to MusicXML, the standard every major notation program reads, so your music is never locked in.
  • Signing in is optional. It adds a cloud library and share links.

Your data

Scores are private unless you choose to share them. We store only what is needed to run your account. See the privacy policy.
