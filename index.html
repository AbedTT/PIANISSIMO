Three steps to your first score

If you can type, you can write music.

Choose a length

1 whole note … 3 quarter … 4 eighth. Add . for a dotted note.

Type the notes

A–G write a note in the nearest octave. Move it with ↑ ↓, sharpen with =.

Listen and share

Space plays it back. Save to your library and send a link, or print a PDF.
