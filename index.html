Ode to Joy: written in Pianissimo

Everything you need to write music

From a first melody to a printed songbook, a class project or a band rehearsal: one app, nothing to install.

⌨

Keyboard-first writing

Pick a length with 1–7, type A–G. Chords, ties, tuplets and up to four voices on a staff are one key away.

♪

Hear every note

About 30 real instrument sounds, a drum kit, chord accompaniment in five styles, a mixer with mute and solo.

🎹

Practise

Slow it down without changing the tempo, swing the eighths, and let wait mode follow you note by note.

🎤

Sing it in

Sing or play one line into the microphone and it becomes notes. A tuner is built in.

📷

Scan a page

Turn a PDF, scan or photo of printed music into a score you can edit, play and transpose, titles and lyrics included. The page stays beside the score and follows your cursor, so checking it bar by bar is quick.

🎧

From a recording

Turn a WAV or MP3 of a sung or played melody, or of piano and guitar chords, into a score with its tempo, key and time signature, even when the tempo speeds up or slows down.

🎸

Guitar and drums

Tablature worked out from your notes, chord diagrams above the symbols, and a drum staff that plays a real kit.

👥

Write together

Invite someone to a score and edit it at the same time, with their cursor in colour. Undo only takes back your own changes.

📚

Teach a class

Set assignments, see who handed in, comment on bars and give work back with a grade and marks.

♥

Share and remix

Send a link anyone can play, embed a player on your site, publish to Explore, and remix what others share.

✈

Works offline

Install it like an app on your computer, tablet or phone. It opens and keeps working without a connection.

💻

The desktop app

Pianissimo for Windows, Mac and Linux: keep any number of scores in My scores without an account, scan with the music scanner it sets up for you, and update in one click. Download it.

📱

On your Android phone

The Android app writes, plays and transposes scores on a phone or tablet, with no connection and no account: My scores stay on the phone, exports go to Downloads, and the piano sound is built in. Get the APK.

⇄

Every format

MusicXML for MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius and Dorico; MIDI in and out; WAV audio; PNG and SVG images; print and PDF.

🌐

In your language

The editor, the guide and this site in English, French, Spanish and German.

Three steps to your first score

If you can type, you can write music.

Choose a length

1 whole note … 3 quarter … 4 eighth. Add . for a dotted note.

Type the notes

A–G write a note in the nearest octave. Move it with ↑ ↓, sharpen with =.

Listen and share

Space plays it back. Save to your library and send a link, or print a PDF.
